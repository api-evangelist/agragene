Published April 10, 2025 Agragene and Associates Insectary have announced a partnership aimed at scaling the production and delivery of sterile insect technology to help manage Spotted Wing Drosophila (SWD), a significant pest affecting fresh berry crops. The technology, branded K NOCK O UT SWD by Agragene, is designed to reduce SWD populations through the release of sterile male insects.
