Side View of New World Screwworm - courtesy of USDA St. Louis (June 25, 2026) – Agragene announces funding from the USDA New World Screwworm Grand Challenge and from the Foundation for Food & Agriculture Research. The grants were awarded through competitive processes by both USDA and FFAR with the former involving hundreds of submissions.
